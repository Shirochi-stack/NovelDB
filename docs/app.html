NovelDB

NovelDB is a book downloader and reader for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS. It provides a book catalog, a download queue, local files, and optional uploads for sharing completed files.

Google Drive uploads

Choose Google Drive in Upload settings and select Sign in to Google. Google asks you to choose an account and grant permission. NovelDB uploads the files you select to that account's Drive and creates share links that anyone with the link can open.

Read the Google Drive privacy policy.
