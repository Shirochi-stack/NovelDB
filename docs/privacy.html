NovelDB Google Drive privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. This policy describes the optional Google Drive integration in NovelDB's desktop and mobile apps.

Permission and use

Google handles account sign-in and your consent. NovelDB requests the drive.file permission to create uploads and manage the files you use with the app. It uses Google Drive data to perform the uploads and sharing actions you request.

Data sent to Google

When you upload, NovelDB sends the selected file contents, upload filename, and any configured destination folder ID directly from your device to Google's Drive API over HTTPS. Google stores the files in the account you authorize. The app creates a reader permission for anyone with the resulting share link. You control which files you upload and who receives the links.

Local storage

NovelDB keeps upload links and history on your device. Desktop stores the Drive refresh token in its local app configuration so it can obtain access tokens for later uploads. iOS stores the refresh token in the system Keychain. Android uses Google Play services to maintain the authorization and keeps a local connection flag. Access tokens are cached in memory.

Google Drive requests travel between your device and Google. Google's handling of your account and files is described in its own privacy policy.

Retention and control

Use Disconnect in Upload settings to clear NovelDB's local connection. You can also revoke NovelDB's authorization through your Google Account's connections settings. Files you uploaded stay in Google Drive until you delete them there. Manage a file's sharing permissions in Drive to change who can access its link.

Google API data policy

NovelDB's use and transfer of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
